# SLA-LENS: Data Access Policy

## Overview

SLA-LENS was developed using a corpus of **40 annotated online L2 Spanish classroom and language-tutoring transcripts** comprising **21,835 sentence-level rows**. The corpus includes instructor and learner turns, timestamps, classroom-event annotations, and learner-error annotations. The designated model-adaptation partition contains **35 transcripts (19,214 rows)**; the held-out silver-evaluation partition contains **five transcripts (2,621 rows)**. One of the held-out transcripts has an **expert-reviewed gold reference (653 rows)**.

The corpus is participant-derived institutional research data. **The original transcripts and their participant-derived annotations are not publicly distributed through this repository.**


## Research access requests

Requests for access to **de-identified transcripts and/or annotations will be allowed on a case-by-case basis** for legitimate research purposes, subject to approval by the institution(s) responsible for the data and any applicable ethics, consent, and data-use requirements. **Access is discretionary and not guaranteed.**

To inquire about access, contact [author email to be added] with:

1. Your name, institutional affiliation, and research role.
2. A brief description of the research question and intended use.
3. Whether you intend to train models on the data or publish examples or derived artifacts.

Additional institutional documentation, ethics approval, or a signed data-use agreement may be required before any materials are shared. 

## Potential conditions of approved access

Where applicable, an approved data-use agreement may restrict:

- Redistribution, public posting, or sharing beyond approved personnel.
- Publication of identifiable quotations, timestamps, or combinations of metadata.
- Use outside the approved research purpose, including secondary model training.
- Release of trained weights or adapters that may retain participant-derived information.
- Retention beyond the authorized period and inadequate data-security controls.


## Citation and inquiries

If approved access leads to a publication, cite the SLA-LENS paper and follow any acknowledgment or citation requirements in the applicable data-use agreement.
